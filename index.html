THE MYTH OF CALLISTO & ARCAS

• A story written in the stars

In Greek mythology, Kallisto was a beautiful nymph associated with the goddess Artemis. After a tragic series of events, she was transformed into a bear. Her son, Arcas, grew up unaware of her true identity. In the end, Zeus placed them among the stars, where they became the constellations Ursa Major and Ursa Minor. Two souls. One story. Forever in the stars.
